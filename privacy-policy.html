Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-14

This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled for the Tarun Dev Utilities Hub (the "Site"). This policy applies only to the Site and its pages; linked projects or external apps may operate under different practices.

1. Scope & Data Minimization

The Site is designed to run client-side utilities. Except where explicitly stated, the Site does not collect or store personal data on a server. Calculations and interactions happen within your browser, and any results are local to your device unless you explicitly share them.

2. Third-party services

The Site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may set and read cookies, and collect information in accordance with Google's privacy practices. We do not control Google's data collection — visit Google Ads Settings and Google's Privacy Policy to learn more and manage ad personalization.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use small amounts of browser storage (for example, to remember a theme preference). These are non-sensitive values stored on your device. If you clear your browser data, these preferences will be removed.

4. Links to Other Projects

Some links on the Site point to other projects or sub-sites. This policy covers only this Site. Linked projects may have their own privacy policies and practices.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the Site code and hosted files up to date. However, no internet-facing site is completely secure — please avoid sharing sensitive personal information through the Site.

6. Children

These utilities are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Contact & Changes

If you have questions about this policy or the Site, contact: contact.tarundev@gmail.com (this address is injected into the Site via client-side script to reduce scraping).

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision.
